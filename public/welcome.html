Notes Embedder

You're all set

  1. Pin the icon. Click the puzzle piece at the top right of the browser, then the pin next to Notes Embedder.
  2. Open any PDF in a tab, like a paper on arXiv or a journal site.
  3. Click the icon, then "Open this PDF in the reader".
  4. Select text to highlight it, then add a note.

PDFs saved on your computer: open the extension settings and turn on "Allow access to file URLs".
